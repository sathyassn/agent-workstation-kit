# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Welcome questions,
criticize ideas rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and give credit for
work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/sathyassn/agent-workstation-kit/security/advisories/new)
form and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Do not include sensitive details in
a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior puts people or the project at
risk.
