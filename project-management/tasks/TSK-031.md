# TSK-031: Swap, zram, tmp and free-space floors

## Description

Add swap and zram sizing as shares of RAM, the `/tmp` policy (disk-backed, or caches redirected off tmpfs), and disk free-space floors with cleanup guidance, each preview-first, backed up and reversible.

## Outcome

After this merges, a Linux machine's swap, zram and `/tmp` follow the best-practice defaults, and low disk space is flagged before it hurts.

## Deliverables

- unit: `internal/units/resourcelimits/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Each policy applies its default when unset and its opt-out works; evidence: tests
- AC-2 Reverting restores the earlier swap, zram and `/tmp` state, with what takes effect only after a reboot stated; evidence: tests
